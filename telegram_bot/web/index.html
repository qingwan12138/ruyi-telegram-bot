Architecture
The Telegram Bot transports decisions without owning workflow business state.
Workflow→Telegram Bot→Telegram→Human→Telegram Bot→Consuming Workflow

          
No business orchestrationTest, package, and PR execution remain in their workflows.
No persistent stateNo callback history, resolved map, database, or retry queue.
No configuration writesRuntime configuration remains environment-managed and read-only.
